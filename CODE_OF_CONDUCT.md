# Code of Conduct

This is a small, friendly place for people who like making music in Fender Studio Pro 8 and like tinkering with it. Many of us are musicians first and technical second. Please keep it that way.

## What we ask

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. No personal attacks, insults, harassment or pile-ons.
- **Be patient.** Questions that sound basic are welcome. Everyone here started somewhere.
- **No piracy or licence-cracking.** Do not ask for, offer or discuss cracked software, keygens, ways around sign-in, activation or subscriptions, or free copies of paid software and plug-ins.
- **Do not share Fender's files.** No program files, skin files, fonts, images or extracted code from Studio Pro. Share your own work, your own settings and your own words.
- **Respect the people who make the program.** This project is a thank-you to the Studio One team, not a replacement.
- **Keep other people's private details private.** No personal information, and no screenshots with account names or keys in them.

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

I (the maintainer) can edit or remove posts and comments, lock a discussion, and block someone who keeps causing trouble. I will try to say why. If you see something that is not okay, use the **Report** option on that post or comment.

Thank you for being decent to each other.
