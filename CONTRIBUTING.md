# How to join in

You do not need to be a programmer. Most of what helps is a clear idea, a vote, or a good bug report.

> Heads up: these mods are completely vibe-coded. I'm not a programmer. I built all of this with AI and I honestly don't know what I'm doing, so expect bugs and things that break. Back up your songs first, use it at your own risk, and if anything acts up, run the uninstaller to go back to stock.

## Share an idea

1. Open **[Discussions](../../discussions)** and press **New discussion**.
2. Pick **Ideas**.
3. Fill in the short form: your idea, the problem it solves, and how you would use it.

One idea per post works best. A real example ("when I record vocals I always...") beats a long feature list.

## Vote on ideas

Open any post in the **Ideas** category and press the up arrow or add a thumbs-up. Then add a comment on **how you would use it**. That tells me more than the vote alone.

## Ask for help

Post in **Q&A**. Say which Studio Pro build you have and which packages you installed.

## Report a bug

Use **[Issues](../../issues)** and press **New issue**. The form asks for:

- Your **Studio Pro build number** (Help > About).
- Which **package(s)** you have installed, and which switches are on.
- **What happened** and **what you expected**.
- The **steps** to make it happen again.
- Whether it still happens when you **hold Shift** while Studio Pro starts (that starts stock Studio Pro).
- The **log file**: open `%LOCALAPPDATA%\StudioMods\studio-pro-x\sandbox.log` (paste that into the address bar of a File Explorer window), and paste the last 30 or so lines into the form.
- A tick to say you **backed up your songs**.

If you cannot tell which package it was, say what you were doing and I will work it out.

## Share your own mod or change

Post it in **Show and tell**: what it does, which Studio Pro build you tested on, a screenshot, and how to try it. Say what licence it has and what models or tools it uses. Please do not post Fender's files, extracted program code, or anything that touches licensing or sign-in.

## Pull requests

Small pull requests are welcome for:

- **Docs**: typos, clearer steps, missing details.
- **The "For your AI agent" guide**: corrections and new facts about how Studio Pro works (say which build you checked).

If you are not sure how to open a pull request, post it in Show and tell instead and I will help. Changes to the mods themselves are best discussed first in **Ideas**.

## Ground rules

Be kind and read the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Nothing here is for cracking, bypassing or sharing licences.
